Privacy Policy — AI Media Factory

Last updated: August 2026

AI Media Factory is a set of internal automation tools used to publish pre-produced video content (relaxation and ambient sound videos) to social media platforms including YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Pinterest, and Threads, on behalf of the channel "Cozy Rain Retreat."

What data we access

Our integration with the Pinterest API is used solely to publish video Pins to boards we own, and to read basic account/board information needed to do so. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data belonging to Pinterest users, followers, or visitors.

Data we store

We store only: the titles and file names of our own videos, publish scheduling information, and API credentials needed to operate our own connected accounts. This information is kept in a private Google Sheets document, accessible only to the account owner.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect analytics on individual Pinterest users, do not use Pinterest data for advertising, and do not share any data with third parties beyond the automation tools (n8n) used to run this process.
